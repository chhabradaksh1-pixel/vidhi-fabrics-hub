## New this week, measured today

### 1. The homepage fetches a 6 MB video nobody scrolls to

Measured, not inferred:

- `weaving-loom.mp4` is **6,059,529 bytes**.
- The `<video>` carries `autoplay loop muted playsinline`, and **no `poster`
  and no `preload` attribute**.
- It sits at y=3,132px in a 10,069px document — roughly a third of the way
  down, well below the fold.
- A network trace of a cold page load shows the browser issuing range requests
  for it **during initial load, before any scrolling**. `<video>` has no
  native lazy-loading, so nothing defers it.
- It is served `cache-control: public, max-age=0, must-revalidate`, so every
  first visit pays the full 6 MB.

Mobile is 80 of 130 impressions in the last export, at a better average
position than desktop. This is the largest avoidable cost on the page for that
audience, by a wide margin — the entire rest of the homepage is 111 KB of HTML
plus 2.1 MB of images that are all lazy-loaded.

**Not fixed here, deliberately.** The obvious change is `preload="none"` plus
starting playback on intersection (the repo already has that pattern in
`useReveal`), with a poster frame behind it. Two reasons to escalate instead:
there is no ffmpeg in this environment so a poster frame cannot be extracted,
and changing when a hero-adjacent video starts is an appearance decision
`CLAUDE.md` reserves for the owner. **Recommend the owner approve this; it is
the highest-value technical change available.**

### 2. Four images lost their intrinsic dimensions this week

`8e589c5` and `5b4d56b` (26 September) introduced the illustrated logo and the
full-bleed footer illustration. Both went in without `width`/`height`:

- the logo `<img>`, in three places — `routes/index.tsx`, `routes/hi.tsx`,
  `components/fabric-desk.tsx` — so it affects every page on the site
- the footer illustration in `components/site-footer.tsx`

Every other `<img>` in the codebase (9 of them) has both attributes. This is a
consistency regression, not a pre-existing pattern.

**Honest caveat: I could not measure a benefit.** CLS on a full scroll-through
at 375×812 measured **0.000 both with and without the attributes** on a local
dev server. Images arrive too fast there for a shift to occur. The attributes
matter when images are slow relative to layout, which is the mid-range-Android
case this site's audience is in and which I cannot reproduce here.

So: correct, free, consistent with the rest of the codebase, and of unproven
value. It is in the PR on that basis, not on a promised Core Web Vitals win.
