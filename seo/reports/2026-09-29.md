### Next in sequence: the six fabric pages (cluster E), blocked on a fact check

`content-strategy.md` says cluster E is ready to build because "the spec data
already exists". It exists, but I don't think it can be published yet.

- Every spec line in `src/content/fabrics.ts` goes back to `45e62d4` (5
  September), the initial "Changes" commit from the site template. Nothing
  shows the owner ever supplied or checked these numbers.
- Two values look wrong. **This is my inference from general textile knowledge.
  I did not measure it:**
  - **Katan Jacquard, 90 momme.** Most silk dress fabric is in the tens of
    momme. At 90, this would be far heavier than any normal katan saree fabric.
  - **Beaded Organza, 40 momme.** Organza is a sheer, lightweight weave, usually
    a few momme. 40 would not be sheer.
  - The four GSM figures (280, 110, 190, 340) are plausible for what they
    describe. They are still unconfirmed.

A page built to rank for "katan jacquard GSM" or similar searches only works if
the number on it is right. The target reader is a trade buyer who knows these
weights, and a wrong figure costs credibility with exactly the people the
page is for. The same numbers are already on both home routes in the catalogue
cards, so this is a live accuracy question as well as a blocker for new pages.

**Ask for the owner:** confirm or correct the weight, width and composition of
the six catalogue fabrics. Also say whether the SKUs (`VF-C-014` etc.) are real
catalogue codes or template placeholders.

Cluster E also adds routes, which makes it a route-tree change. Under
